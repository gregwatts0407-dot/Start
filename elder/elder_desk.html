Elder Triple Screen Desk

The rulebook

Screen 1 · Tide · weeklyWeekly Impulse gives permission

Green (13-week EMA and weekly MACD-histogram both rising) permits longs only. Red permits shorts only. Blue permits no new trades. Only the last completed week counts.

Screen 2 · Wave · dailyTrade the pullback against the tide

For a long, the 2-day EMA of Force Index dips below zero and the daily Impulse is not red. For a short, it rises above zero and the daily Impulse is not green. Bear/Bull Power and the 13/26-day value zone grade the setup.

Screen 3 · EntryTrailing buy stop or value limit

Place a buy stop one tick above the setup bar's high and trail it lower each day until filled. Without a pullback, bid inside the value zone between the 13 and 26-day EMAs, never into strength.

Exits & moneySafeZone, channel, 2% and 6%

Initial stop below the last two lows. It trails up with the SafeZone stop (2.5× the average 10-day penetration) and is never lowered. Take profit at the Autoenvelope line. Risk 2% per long, 1% per short, and stop trading for the month at a 6% drawdown.

Backtest of these exact rules · 5 years of daily data

Method and caveats

Forecast cone. The cone is built from historical analogs, not a fitted model. Every past day in the same Elder state (weekly Impulse × daily Impulse) gives its next 30 bars, measured in units of that day's 20-day volatility. These paths are rescaled to today's volatility. The bands are the 10–90th and 25–75th percentiles, and the line is the median. "Target before stop" counts the analog paths that fill the order and resolve within 30 bars, using daily closes only.

Data. Daily OHLCV comes from Yahoo Finance via the repo's GitHub Actions workflow. Gold uses front-month COMEX futures (GC=F) as a proxy for spot XAU/USD, which typically trades a few dollars below the futures price. Bars still forming on the as-of date are excluded for gold and Bitcoin. ASX 200 volume on Yahoo is unreliable, which weakens its Force Index.

Limits. Weekly-tide strictness and short-side risk were chosen after comparing eight rule variants on this same 5-year window, so the backtest is in-sample and optimistic. It ignores costs, slippage and gaps through stops beyond the open. This is a research tool, not financial advice.
