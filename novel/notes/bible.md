# THE SAME NIGHT, SEVEN WAYS — working bible (continuity reference; contains every spoiler)

Target: ~250,000 words. 7 parts x 7 chapters x ~5,100 words + editor's notes (~700 each) + prologue.
Files: novel/manuscript/NN-*.md, assembled in filename order by novel/build.py.

## PREMISE
Night of Sat 22 / Sun 23 October 1994, the night the clocks went back. Compline House, on a tidal island (Compline Ness, "the Ness") in the Tolle estuary, Essex. Edmund Vane (52) is found drowned two days later. Six adults each privately believe they killed him; each left an account, each in the genre they live in. Seventh witness = a child nobody knew was in the house (Wren Garrow, 9¾), who as an adult (39, in 2024) edits the accounts and, at a last dinner at Compline on the clock-change night 26/27 Oct 2024, makes them fit. Truth: Edmund survived every one of the six "murders" and drowned saving Wren on the causeway — exactly as his mother Marguerite drowned saving HIM, in the same hour, in 1974, a thing he never told.
RULE: no narrator lies. Every literal perception is accurate. Contradictions come from (a) three clocks, (b) inference, (c) doubles (two dresses, two Thorne clocks, a wooden man), (d) where each story stops.

## THE THREE CLOCKS
- W = world time, unadjusted BST (Cade's hidden wristwatch; Tom's pocket watch; Wren's Casio). MASTER for this bible.
- H = house time = GMT = W minus 1 hour. Compline has refused summer time since 1974. All 111 house clocks, incl. stable tower clock. Used by Leo, Hester, Toby, Imogen, kitchen.
- At W 02:00 the world goes back to 01:00 and agrees with the house. The hour the world lives twice = H 1:00–2:00 = "the Odd Hour" (Marguerite's tradition since the 1950s: what is said in the Odd Hour is off the ledger, never held against you). Leo opens the front door at H 1:00 "to let the world come home".
- Guests surrender watches into "the Font" (pewter christening basin on the hall table). Cade keeps his (strapped above the elbow? no — in his sock). 
- So "half past one" = two moments an hour apart; "midnight" likewise (Tom's midnight W = on the Wade; Imogen's midnight H = W 01:00 in the orangery).

## TIDE (W)
High water 01:52 W, springs plus North Sea surge, SW gale backing NW. The Wade impassable on foot 22:05–05:35. A horse that knows it: till ~23:40 and from ~03:40 (swims the Rill). Slack ~01:40–02:05 (only time a boat can lie at the Hythe).

## THE THORNE SIDEREAL
Table regulator, 1771, Isaac Thorne of Colchester (fictional): mean time, sidereal time, equation, moon, and tide at Compline Bar. Bought by Leo as a wreck, Norwich 1951; restored 9 years. Est. £1.2m. Maker's mark: a thorn sprig scratched on the back of the moon disc and under the dial foot. THE COPY: Leo's teaching replica (1962–70), perfect, but no thorn sprig. In Sept 1994 Leo (lucid, suspicious) moved the REAL one to the tower clock-room cupboard and coupled it to the turret clock with brass rods ("the Reconciler"); the COPY went into the library case. Edmund crates the COPY (believing it real) at W 23:25 → ice house → Toby carries crate to kitchen thinking it the bombe box, opens it at the ceremony ("Ah, the Copy," says Leo) → pantry → Cade & Celia drive off with it → Celia sells it to Sears's buyer as real. Real one found in tower Nov 1994; British Museum 1996. Its winding key was in Edmund's dinner-jacket pocket, which Wren kept.

## 1974 — THE TRUTH (told only on the Perch, Part VII ch.6)
Marguerite meant to leave Leo for Amos in the Odd Hour. Only Imogen knew (helped her pack); Imogen blurted it to Edmund in a quarrel. Edmund (32, drunk) followed his mother onto the Wade. She was almost across — Amos saw her lantern — when Edmund caught her; they argued an hour; he sat down on the Clapper and would not move; the flood came. She could have walked on 200 yards and lived. She turned back for her son, got him up the Perch, and was taken. He walked home at dawn and said nothing. So: Leo believes his early clock-change killed her as she left him; Amos believed she changed her mind and chose Leo; Imogen believes her blurting sent Edmund to delay her fatally; each wrong. After it Leo never changed a Compline clock again. Edmund kept her wedding ring 20 years.

V WESTERN (Tom, set down by the editor "as he told it"): spare third person, "the man", NO quotation marks, landscape, laconic.
VI SCIENTIFIC ROMANCE (Leo, dictated to Hester winter 94–95): Wellsian, numbered sections, formal; memory slips rendered as "leakage" between years.
VII MYSTERY (Wren): the child's notebook verbatim + adult plain prose; the summation; the Perch.

Sears's men beat Cade in January: clock is a copy "made about 1965". Real one found in tower clock room spring 1995 after Leo's death (March). Postcard from Portugal 1996: "It isn't the right time here either."
- DI Brand. Inquest Dec, Maldon: drowning, alive on entering water, ~36 hrs immersed; depressed fracture L temporal (hours before death); later contusion occiput; 11 pellets No.5 L shoulder/neck/ear from below; chloral substantial; evening trousers, dress shirt, braces, ONE SOCK, no jacket; abrasions palms/forearms with TARRED WOOD FIBRES. Open verdict; coroner "hoped somebody's conscience would supply what the evidence did not"; everyone looked down. Tom sat beside Imogen.
